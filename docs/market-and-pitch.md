# Market, Pitch & Positioning

_Uncommitted working doc. Sources cited in the research; figures marked estimate-grade where applicable._

## The investable syllogism

1. **Prediction markets are the hottest fundable category in fintech.** Polymarket: $2B+ from ICE (NYSE's
   parent) at ~$9B, reportedly raising at $15B. Kalshi: $2B → reported $40B valuation in ~14 months (a16z,
   Sequoia, Paradigm, Coatue money in the category).
2. **Nigeria is the world's top grassroots crypto/stablecoin market.** ~$92B received (2025 report period),
   stablecoins ~43% of regional volume, IMF now tracks Nigerian stablecoin flows as a named channel. ~18.8M
   Nigerians bet (2024), ~90% via smartphone; Bet9ja claims 15M+ users; #1 iGaming market in Africa (online
   market ~$500M+/yr, estimate-grade).
3. **Nobody has built the intersection, and no incumbent can retrofit the key feature.** Polymarket positions
   are fully public (a whole tool ecosystem tracks/copies whale wallets); Kalshi is KYC-visible to the operator
   and state. Local players (Bayse, Luno x Limitless, BlockForecast) are not privacy-preserving.

## Why privacy is load-bearing in Nigeria (documented, citable)

- #EndSARS (2020): CBN froze ~20 protest organizers' bank accounts; Feminist Coalition pivoted to bitcoin.
- CBN crypto ban (2021): banks ordered to close crypto users' accounts; P2P boomed instead; reversed Dec 2023.
- Binance crackdown (2024): executive detained ~8 months; state blamed crypto for the naira's fall.
- EFCC (2024): court-approved freezing of **1,146 bank accounts** over FX/crypto P2P activity.
- eNaira flopped partly on surveillance fears; cNGN (SEC-supervised naira stablecoin) launched Feb 2025, not on
  Starknet.

Pitch line: "On Polymarket, this exact position gets you copy-traded. In Nigeria, it can get you frozen."

## The macro pain = the market seed

Fuel subsidy removal took petrol from ₦185 to ₦650+/litre; the naira went ~₦450/$ to ~₦1,035/$ in a year;
inflation hit ~35% (Nov 2024), food ~40%. Every Nigerian already holds a view on next month's rate and pump
price. BBNaija drew 1.53B votes in a season; football betting is mainstream. Economic markets are the wedge;
culture/sports are the growth loop.

## Competition snapshot

- Polymarket: accessible from Nigeria, no local rails, public positions, runs Nigeria-topic markets.
- Luno x Limitless (2026): prediction product in Nigeria, USDC, custodial, not private.
- Bayse Markets (ex-Gowagr), BlockForecast (Base): early, not private.
- Hackathon lane: blindpool (starred, live demo) and Veilcast build generic "invisible bettors" markets; our
  differentiation is the Nigeria wedge + parimutuel + trustless Pragma resolution + documented naira methodology.

## Regulatory honesty (one paragraph)

Nigeria's gaming law moved to state level after the Supreme Court nullified the National Lottery Act (Nov 2024);
online operators sit in acknowledged limbo. The SEC's crypto regime (ARIP/ISA 2025) licenses VASPs slowly. No
Nigerian regulator has addressed prediction markets. Position as information/forecasting markets (Kalshi's
framing), expect privacy to be read through an AML lens, and answer with selective disclosure and screening
compatibility rather than ignoring it. Enforcement risk in Nigeria is discretionary and can precede legal
clarity; this is a prod-stage workstream.

## Positioning

**Core frame: a prediction market built on Nigerian specifics, open to the world.** Nigeria is the content
wedge, not a user restriction: locals, the 17M+ diaspora, and global macro traders all take positions on
Nigeria-native questions. Language discipline: participants take **positions** and make **predictions**; we
never say "bet" or "gambling" (forecasting/information-market framing, which is also the regulatory posture).

**Market catalog (the moat is the content):** Economy (inflation, naira rate, fuel price), Policy (border
openings/closings, subsidy decisions), Resources (oil and minerals, production and benchmarks), Culture and
trends (influencer questions like "will Peller be gifted another car?", viral-trend timing, BBNaija), Sports.
Culture markets require crisply worded resolution criteria with named evidence sources committed at creation.

- **For hackathon judges:** privacy is load-bearing, evidenced by 1,146 frozen accounts, not a checkbox. Deep
  native integration: private positions via the pool's invoke action, winnings as encrypted notes, honest leakage
  table, trustless Pragma resolution.
- **For investors:** a $22 to 40B-validated category × the world's #1 grassroots stablecoin market × content no
  global platform covers × the one feature incumbents structurally cannot copy. v1 proves the mechanic on
  mainnet; v2A (naira onramp) opens the mass market; v2B (progressive decentralization) removes trust.
